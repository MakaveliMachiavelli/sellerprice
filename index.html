FAQ

Which fees does this include?

Commission %, transaction/payment %, and any fixed service fee — plus shipping you absorb and vouchers you give, which are the hidden margin-killers. Enter rates from your Seller Center (they vary by category and promo).

How does the suggested price formula work?

price = (cost + fixed + ship − voucher) ÷ (1 − margin − commission% − txn%). It's the algebraic inverse of the fee stack — the price that lands your exact target margin.
